Why HARVEST Matters

HARVEST is the difference between a pile of files and a system that keeps learning after the session ends.

  1. You work with an agent Ideas, tasks, decisions, drafts, corrections, and loose threads pile up in a live session.
  2. HARVEST runs on a schedule A cron trigger wakes up the system after you stop working.
  3. It reads sessions and identifies change What got decided? What moved? What should become durable context?
  4. Wiki pages get created or updated Fresh knowledge stops living only inside a transcript.
  5. Connections form New pages get linked back to existing people, projects, tools, and routines.
  6. Next session starts smarter The next model arrives pre-briefed instead of re-learning everything from scratch.
Current step: raw work is entering the system.

Click the button or scroll the steps to animate the loop.

Without HARVEST, your Second Brain is just a file cabinet. WITH HARVEST, it's a living system that learns while you sleep.
